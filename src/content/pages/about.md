Welcome to **The Funhavers**! This is an independent space dedicated to tech writing, outdoor adventures, photography, and software architecture.

I'm Timothy M. Johnson—a technical writer by trade, long-distance runner, avid traveler, and builder of quiet, fast web experiences. Here you'll find long-form essays on technical architecture, dispatches and photo stories from our trips on the road, and notes on life.
